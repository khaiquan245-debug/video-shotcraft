# Asset sources, rights and evidence limits

## Archival photographs and footage
None used. Local projects/assets were inspected first: the only explicitly permitted Solway family photograph is unrelated to these seven cases; Dyatlov packages contain source links/scripts rather than licensed photos. Existing render frames are illustrations, not archives. No unrelated photo was repurposed.

Direct requests to Malaysia MOT, Yale collections and Wikimedia Commons returned proxy CONNECT HTTP403. This prevents current official-status checking and obtaining/checking image reproduction terms here. No restricted proxy was bypassed. The brief explicitly authorizes clearly illustrated substitutes when archival images cannot be used legally. All final scenes are original illustrations/diagrams, with persistent visible disclosure and object-specific disclaimers. No fake archival photograph, copied facsimile, victim photograph, fabricated cockpit dialogue or confirmed landing of Cooper is used.
