# Title
He Survived 62 Hours Underwater | Harrison Okene

# Description
Harrison Okene survived 62 hours trapped inside a sunken ship, nearly 100 feet beneath the ocean. When rescue divers entered the wreck, they expected to find bodies. Then a hand moved.

In May 2013, the tugboat Jascon-4 capsized off Nigeria. The ship's cook survived in an air pocket until rescuers discovered him alive. His recovery required a pressurized rescue and decompression treatment.

An illustrated reconstruction of a real survival story, featuring 2D/2.5D animation.
