# WHAT IF YOU COULD SEE 10 SECONDS INTO THE FUTURE?

**Estimated length:** 7–8 minutes
**Style:** Mysterious – funny – suspenseful – emotional
**Narration:** Fast and intriguing at the beginning, gradually becoming more emotional toward the climax.

---

## SCENE 1 — HOOK: WHAT COULD 10 SECONDS CHANGE?
### 0:00 – 0:30

**[Visuals / Shots]**

Black screen.

The sound of a clock ticking.

A red countdown appears:

**10… 9… 8…**

Quick cuts:

A cup falling off a table.

A car speeding around a blind corner.

A girl about to respond to a confession.

A man stepping into the street.

A phone screen showing:

**MOM CALLING**

Countdown:

**3… 2… 1…**

Freeze frame.

**[Voice-over]**

What if, right now…

you could see exactly what was going to happen in the next **10 seconds**?

What would you do?

Avoid an embarrassing fall?

Prevent an accident?

Find out what your crush is about to say?

Or…

use those 10 seconds to change something you might regret for the rest of your life?

It sounds like a pretty small superpower.

But think about it.

**Ten seconds can sometimes change everything.**

**[Sound / Music]**

Clock ticking grows louder.

Deep bass hit on the final sentence.

---

## SCENE 2 — HOW DOES THE POWER WORK?
### 0:30 – 1:00

**[Visuals / Shots]**

You stand in front of a mirror.

You blink.

The screen flashes.

Everything becomes slightly blue-tinted.

Text appears:

**+10 SECONDS**

Split screen:

**FUTURE / NOW**

**[Voice-over]**

The rule is simple.

Whenever you focus and blink…

you can see exactly **10 seconds into the future**.

Not a feeling.

Not a prediction.

You actually see what is about to happen.

Then…

you return to the present.

And you have 10 seconds to decide:

let that future happen…

or try to change it.

At first?

You would obviously use this incredible power for the most important things in life.

Like…

not embarrassing yourself.

**[Visual Effect]**

REWIND.

---

## SCENE 3 — SITUATION 1: DODGING THE MOST EMBARRASSING FALL EVER
### 1:00 – 1:35

**[Visuals / Shots]**

You are walking down the sidewalk holding a cup of coffee.

You blink.

Ten seconds into the future:

Your foot catches on a crack.

The coffee flies through the air.

You fall straight onto the ground in front of a group of people.

One of them immediately pulls out a phone.

**[Voice-over]**

You see yourself trip.

And somehow…

the entire cup of coffee ends up all over your clothes.

Even worse…

someone nearby is already holding a phone.

No.

The internet does not need another meme today.

You return to the present.

Step slightly to the left.

Avoid the crack.

Perfect.

You smile confidently.

Having a superpower feels amazing.

Then…

you walk straight into a pole.

**BONK!**

Apparently…

seeing the future still cannot save your dignity.

**[Sound / Music]**

Light comedy music.

Cartoon “bonk” sound.

---

## SCENE 4 — SITUATION 2: YOUR BOSS ASKS YOU A QUESTION
### 1:35 – 2:10

**[Visuals / Shots]**

Office meeting.

Your boss looks around the room.

You blink.

Future vision:

Your boss points directly at you.

“What do you think about this plan?”

You freeze.

**[Voice-over]**

During a meeting…

you suddenly see your boss calling your name.

You have exactly 10 seconds.

Your brain instantly enters emergency mode.

You open the document.

Scan the most important line.

Ten seconds later…

“What do you think?”

You sit up straight.

Stay calm.

And give a surprisingly intelligent answer.

Everyone nods.

Your boss looks impressed.

Congratulations.

You just used a supernatural ability to look like someone…

who was actually paying attention during the meeting.

And honestly…

that might be one of the best uses of this power.

---

## SCENE 5 — SITUATION 3: SAVING YOUR PHONE
### 2:10 – 2:40

**[Visuals / Shots]**

Your phone is sitting dangerously close to the edge of a table.

A cat walks toward it.

You blink.

Future vision:

The cat touches the phone.

It falls.

Screen first.

Crack.

**[Voice-over]**

Sometimes the power would help with much smaller things.

You see your phone about to fall off the table.

The screen hits the floor.

Shatters.

You return to the present.

Calmly reach out your hand.

The cat pushes the phone.

And…

you catch it.

Perfect.

Your superpower just saved you a few hundred dollars in repairs.

But from this point on…

things start getting much more serious.

---

## SCENE 6 — SITUATION 4: TEN SECONDS ON THE ROAD
### 2:40 – 3:25

**[Visuals / Shots]**

You are standing at an intersection.

The light turns green.

You are about to move.

You blink.

Future vision:

A motorcycle speeds through the red light from the right.

A collision happens directly in front of you.

Countdown:

**00:07**

**[Voice-over]**

You are about to cross the street.

The light is green.

Everything looks completely normal.

Then you blink.

And see…

a vehicle speeding out from a blind corner.

If you keep moving…

you are going to collide.

You return to the present.

And stop.

One second.

Two seconds.

Three.

The vehicle actually flies past you.

Only a few feet away.

And for the first time…

you realize something.

This power is not just useful for avoiding embarrassing moments.

It could save your life.

Maybe even someone else’s.

---

## SCENE 7 — SITUATION 5: SPORTS
### 3:25 – 4:05

**[Visuals / Shots]**

A soccer match.

You face another player.

You blink.

You see him move to the right.

You return to the present.

You immediately move right before he does.

But he notices and cuts left instead.

**[Voice-over]**

And of course…

you would immediately think about sports.

During a game…

you see your opponent move to the right.

Easy.

You return to the present…

and move right before he even makes his move.

But there is a problem.

He sees you move.

So he changes direction.

Left.

Wait.

That is not what you saw.

And that is when you discover the biggest problem with your power.

**The future is not fixed.**

The moment you change what you do…

the future changes too.

Every decision creates a different outcome.

---

## SCENE 8 — SITUATION 6: THE JOB INTERVIEW
### 4:05 – 4:40

**[Visuals / Shots]**

You sit across from an interviewer.

Your hands are slightly nervous.

You blink.

Future vision:

“What is your biggest weakness?”

You struggle to answer.

**[Voice-over]**

During a job interview…

you see the next question.

“What is your biggest weakness?”

You have 10 seconds to prepare.

This time…

no awkward silence.

No panic.

You deliver the perfect answer.

The interviewer smiles.

But then you look 10 seconds ahead again.

And hear him ask:

“Did you prepare that answer beforehand?”

…

Okay.

Maybe this power cannot save you from every interview.

---

## SCENE 9 — SITUATION 7: CONFESSING YOUR FEELINGS
### 4:40 – 5:30

**[Visuals / Shots]**

Late afternoon.

You are standing in front of someone you like.

You gather your courage.

Blink.

Future vision:

You say:

“I like you.”

She pauses.

Then says:

“I’m sorry…”

You return to the present.

**[Voice-over]**

But some things are much harder.

You are standing in front of someone you have liked for months.

The words have been stuck in your head forever.

You blink.

And see her reaction.

“I’m sorry…”

Your heart drops.

Now you have a choice.

You could stay silent.

Say nothing.

Never get rejected.

Never feel the pain.

But then you start thinking…

if you avoid every future you do not like…

are you really living?

You take a breath.

And say it anyway.

“I like you.”

She pauses.

Then…

“I’m sorry. I’m just not ready for a relationship.”

Exactly what you saw.

But this time…

you smile.

Because at least now…

you have an answer.

Some futures do not need to be changed.

Sometimes…

you just need enough courage to face them.

---

## SCENE 10 — CLIMAX: THE PHONE CALL
### 5:30 – 6:50

**[Visuals / Shots]**

Night.

You are walking home.

Your phone vibrates.

**MOM CALLING**

You look at the screen.

You are busy.

You almost decline the call.

Then you blink.

Future vision:

You press “Decline.”

Keep walking.

Behind you…

an elderly man steps into the street.

A truck speeds toward him.

Brakes screech.

Screen goes black.

You immediately return to the present.

**[Voice-over — slower]**

Then one day…

those 10 seconds really change you.

Your phone starts ringing.

It is your mom.

You are busy.

You think:

“I’ll call her back later.”

Your finger moves toward the decline button.

But out of habit…

you look 10 seconds into the future.

You see yourself rejecting the call.

Walking forward.

And behind you…

an elderly man steps into the road.

A truck is coming straight toward him.

You return to the present.

You do not think.

You run.

Grab the man.

Pull him backward.

The truck flies past.

Missing both of you by inches.

You are breathing hard.

The man does not even understand what just happened.

You just saved someone’s life.

Then…

your phone vibrates again.

Your mom is still calling.

You answer.

“Mom?”

There is a brief silence.

Then she simply asks:

“Did you eat yet?”

That is it.

No emergency.

No terrible news.

No dramatic final words.

Just an ordinary question.

The kind of question you might ignore every day.

You laugh.

“Yeah, Mom. I already ate.”

You talk for a few minutes.

Nothing dramatic happens.

But suddenly…

you realize something.

You spent the entire day staring **10 seconds into the future**…

and nearly ignored someone who was with you…

**right now.**

**[Sound / Music]**

Soft piano begins.

The ticking stops.

---

## SCENE 11 — THE TWIST: YOU STILL CANNOT SEE EVERYTHING
### 6:50 – 7:20

**[Visuals / Shots]**

Montage of previous scenes:

The fall.

The meeting.

The phone.

The traffic accident.

The confession.

The call from Mom.

Then the 10-second countdown disappears.

**[Voice-over]**

You thought the greatest part of your power…

was knowing what would happen next.

But it was not.

Because even if you can see 10 seconds ahead…

you still do not know what happens in 10 minutes.

Tomorrow.

Next year.

Or when the last time you see someone…

will actually be.

And maybe…

that is exactly what gives life its meaning.

---

## SCENE 12 — ENDING
### 7:20 – 8:00

**[Visuals / Shots]**

An ordinary day.

You walk through the city.

A family has dinner together.

Friends laugh.

A child runs toward his parents.

Sunset.

No future vision.

No countdown.

Only the present.

**[Voice-over]**

Of course…

seeing 10 seconds into the future is only a **thought experiment**.

It is not something we can actually do.

In real life…

there is no rewind button.

No warning screen.

No countdown telling us that an important moment is about to happen.

But maybe…

we do not need one.

You can still look before crossing the street.

You can still say what you really feel.

You can call your parents.

Apologize to someone.

Thank someone.

Hold someone you love for a little longer.

And sometimes…

small decisions like those are enough to change the future.

You do not need to see 10 seconds ahead to live a better life.

You just need to stop wasting…

**the 10 seconds you have right now.**

Black screen.

Text appears:

**“The future can wait. This moment can’t.”**

Then:

**If you really could see 10 seconds into the future…
what would you use it for?**

**[Sound / Music]**

Soft piano.

The ticking slowly returns.

**10… 9… 8…**

Fade to black.
