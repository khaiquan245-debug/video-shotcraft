# PRIORITY 4 — REBUILD SUBTITLES USING MEANINGFUL PHRASES

The current subtitles are awkwardly divided by character count.

Examples of incorrect breaks:

"From a mountain with too little visible"

"Roughly thirty surviving bronze gears lie / inside"

"demanded two hundred thousand dollars and four"

These are unacceptable.

## NEW SUBTITLE RULES

1. Synchronize captions with actual spoken audio.
2. Split subtitles by semantic phrase, not raw character count.
3. Prefer punctuation or natural speech pauses.
4. Never separate a number from the noun it describes.
5. Never leave a dangling preposition or conjunction.
6. Never isolate words such as "inside," "and four," or "visible" without their relevant phrase.
7. Maximum two lines per subtitle.
8. Avoid overly long lines.
9. Keep captions visually balanced.
10. Preserve exact meaning even when displayed numbers use digits.

## CORRECT SEGMENTATION EXAMPLES

Incorrect:

"Roughly thirty surviving bronze gears lie"

"inside the fragments of a device"

Better:

"Roughly 30 surviving bronze gears"

"lie inside the fragments of a device."

Incorrect:

"From a mountain with too little visible"

"debris, the trail leads..."

Better:

"From a mountain with too little visible debris,"

"the trail leads to a devastated forest."

Incorrect:

"He demanded two hundred thousand dollars and four"

"parachutes."

Better:

"He demanded $200,000"

"and four parachutes."

Create each caption's boundaries according to the actual narration timing.

Do not force every sentence into only one caption.
