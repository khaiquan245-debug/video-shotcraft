Use the following cinematic techniques throughout the video:

- Establishing wide shots to introduce locations.
- Medium shots for character actions.
- Close-ups for emotional reactions.
- Extreme close-ups for important discoveries.
- Over-the-shoulder shots for studying, coding, and business tasks.
- Top-down shots for books, desks, money, and documents.

- Match cuts and visual transitions between related concepts.

Avoid unnecessary spinning cameras, random zooms, shaking, excessive transitions, or flashy effects that distract from the narration.

Every camera movement must have a storytelling purpose.

# 7. FINAL EDITING REQUIREMENTS

Ensure the finished video has the following professional qualities.

### Storytelling

- Follow the scene order exactly.
- Include every scene and the complete voice-over.
- Do not summarize or skip sections.
- Maintain logical continuity between events.
- Preserve the narrative progression from excitement to opportunity, worldwide attention, responsibility, and emotional reflection.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Keep the audience engaged through visual storytelling, not just narration.

### Animation

- Animate characters throughout the video.
- Avoid long sequences of completely static images.
- Use gestures and reactions that match the spoken lines.
- Keep backgrounds stable and logically consistent.
- Use smooth motion and natural animation timing.
- Avoid distorted hands, duplicated limbs, changing faces, and inconsistent clothing.
- Maintain Stan's exact appearance throughout all scenes.

### Cinematography

- Use different shot sizes and camera angles.
- Keep camera movements smooth.
- Use fast editing for study and business montages.
- Use slower editing for emotional scenes.
- Make transitions visually motivated and consistent.
- Do not rely on repetitive zoom-in and zoom-out effects.

### Audio

- Synchronize narration with the animation.
- Keep one consistent narrator throughout the entire video.
- Ensure narration is clearly audible.
- Lower background music automatically under speech.
- Include sound effects only when they support visible actions.
- Avoid abrupt music changes.
- Use silence intentionally during important dramatic moments.

### Narrative accuracy

- Learning 100 times faster does not mean thinking, moving, or working physically 100 times faster.
- The character cannot predict the future or read minds.
- Faster learning does not automatically guarantee wealth or success.
- Unknown scientific discoveries still require research and experimentation.
