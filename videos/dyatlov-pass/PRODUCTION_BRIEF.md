## 2. HISTORICAL ACCURACY

This is a REAL historical incident.

In early February 1959, nine experienced hikers led by Igor Dyatlov died in the northern Ural Mountains of the Soviet Union.

A tenth member, Yuri Yudin, had turned back earlier because of illness and survived.

The group took photographs during the expedition. A photograph taken while preparing their final campsite is documented by scientific researchers.

Rescuers later discovered their damaged tent, which had been cut from the inside. Equipment, boots, and warm clothing remained at the campsite.

Some hikers died from hypothermia, and some had severe traumatic injuries.

Research published in 2021 proposed that a small slab avalanche could explain many of the circumstances. The exact sequence of events remains uncertain.

Do not invent supernatural events, monsters, secret weapons, eyewitness testimony, or fictional discoveries.

Treat avalanche explanations as scientific hypotheses supported by research, not a complete reconstruction proven beyond doubt.
